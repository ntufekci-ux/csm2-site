Our position

We are not the EPC contractor. We are not the lender.

CSM2 Finance structures the transaction so both can engage on bankable, executable terms. A financing idea is not a financing solution: capital becomes credible only when the project behind it is technically mature, commercially coherent, properly packaged and executable.

Most advisory firms stop at the financing structure. We go further by connecting it with technical maturity, procurement strategy, contract packaging and implementation planning.

When is a project EPC+F-ready?

We screen every transaction early against the six issues that drive lender and ECA appetite.

A project does not need to be perfect at screening. It needs a credible route to close the gaps.

01
Technical scope
Is the capacity, design basis and implementation scope sufficiently defined to price and diligence?
02
Borrower / sponsor
Who borrows, who owns the asset, and what balance-sheet or sovereign support exists?
03
Repayment source
What ultimately repays the debt: public budget, corporate cash flow, tariff, PPA, off-take or escrow?
04
ECA / export content
Can the supply chain create eligible export value without distorting the technical solution?
05
ESG / permits / land
Are material environmental, social, land, permitting and compliance risks on a credible path?
06
Contract & execution
Can completion, performance, interfaces, schedule and payment milestones be allocated bankably?

Financing routes

The route is selected around the transaction, not the other way around.

The appropriate structure depends on the borrower, repayment source, country risk, eligible procurement content, security package and delivery model.

Sovereign / public
Ministry, public agency or state-owned borrower with sovereign guarantee, budget support or public repayment source.
Corporate / balance sheet
Private borrower supported by corporate credit, parent guarantee, assets, cash flow and covenant package.
Project / limited recourse
SPV debt supported primarily by project cash flows, off-take, concession rights and project-level security.
ECA-supported / blended
Buyer's credit, export credit cover, DFI participation, risk insurance or blended structures layered onto public or private debt.
ECA / DFI support is not a separate universe: it overlays the borrower and repayment structure and is shaped by sourcing, country and risk allocation.
