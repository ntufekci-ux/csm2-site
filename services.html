Services

Services that turn projects into financeable transactions.

A financing model is only as strong as the project assumptions behind it. We review the technical and procurement basis of a project before it is translated into lender-facing financial documentation.

01

EPC+F & ECA/DFI Structuring

Selecting and developing the financing route around the borrower, repayment source and eligible content.

— EPC+F structure development — ECA/DFI route assessment — Buyer's credit, supplier credit and project finance options — Lender and ECA interface strategy — Transaction framework preparation — Financing assumptions and eligibility logic
02

Financial Modelling & Bankability

A financial model is not only an Excel file: it is the lender-facing statement of the project.

— Financial model development and review — CAPEX and OPEX assumptions — Revenue and repayment logic — Sensitivity analysis and financing scenarios — Bankability package — Information memorandum and lender presentation support
03

Engineering & Technical Advisory

Testing the technical basis before it is translated into financial documentation.

— Technical scope review — Design maturity assessment — CAPEX validation — Technical risk identification — Interface review between design, procurement and implementation — Review of technical assumptions used in financial models — Technical documentation support for lenders
04

Procurement & Contract Packaging

Aligning the supply chain and contract structure with financing eligibility.

— Procurement strategy review — Supplier and contractor coordination — Contract packaging — Origin and content alignment for ECA eligibility — Equipment and service package structuring — Tender and procurement documentation support — Coordination between owner, EPC contractor, suppliers and financing parties
05

Project Management & Implementation Readiness

Building financing parties' confidence that the project can be delivered.

— Implementation strategy — Project execution planning — Schedule logic and milestone review — Stakeholder coordination — Risk register and mitigation planning — Reporting and project control framework

What lenders actually test

The bankability levers that shape EPC+F success.

A strong financing structure is the result of aligned project fundamentals, not a substitute for them.

Technical & CAPEX
Scope maturity, design basis, CAPEX defensibility, implementation risk.
Contract structure
Completion, performance, interfaces, liquidated damages, security and guarantees.
ECA / export content
Eligible sourcing, origin rules, local content, supplier structure.
Payment / revenue
Repayment source, tariff, PPA or budget, milestones, escrow and public support.
Risk mitigation & debt terms
Political and FX risk, insurance, guarantees, reserves; tenor, pricing, amortization, syndication appetite.
ESG & compliance
Land, permits, climate alignment, E&S, integrity, reporting and procurement rules.
