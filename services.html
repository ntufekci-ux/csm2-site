Questions

Asked in first meetings.

What does CSM2 Finance actually do?

We structure the transaction: technical scope, procurement content, contracts, risk allocation and debt structure aligned into one lender-facing package. We are not the EPC contractor and we are not the lender.

What does EPC+F really mean?

Turnkey delivery logic combined with financing structuring, so the project is developed as one bankable transaction architecture, not as a construction contract with a financing line attached later.

Does a project have to be fully developed?

No. A project does not need to be perfect at screening. It needs a credible route to close the gaps.

What do you need to screen a project?

Project location, sector, estimated investment size, development stage, expected procurement route and financing objective.

Are you tied to a technology or supplier?

No. Our role is technology-neutral and sponsor- and transaction-focused. Supplier origin is tested against ECA rules, not chosen in advance.

Do you stay involved after financial close?

We can. Many mandates stop when the loan documents are signed; we can continue into drawdown, documentation, contractor interfaces and compliance.
