Working Across the Project Table

Where We Stand

Complex infrastructure and energy projects are shaped by many parties before a financing package is formally approved. CSM2 Finance supports clients by aligning the expectations of project owners, contractors, suppliers, lenders, ECAs, DFIs, public authorities, insurers and advisors around a coherent and financeable project structure.

This is particularly important in EPC-F and ECA-backed transactions, where bankability depends not only on the financial model, but also on contract structure, export content, procurement route, implementation plan, ESG requirements, risk allocation and the credibility of the delivery framework.

Access to the Infrastructure Finance Ecosystem

CSM2 Finance works within a broad international infrastructure finance ecosystem covering export credit agencies, development finance institutions, multilateral development banks, commercial lenders, insurers, risk partners, sponsors, utilities, sovereign counterparts and specialist advisors.

For EPC-F and ECA-backed projects, early alignment with this ecosystem is often decisive. It helps test country appetite, export-content eligibility, tenor, security package, risk coverage, ESG expectations, procurement constraints and the realistic route to financial close.

Through its senior team and partner network, CSM2 Finance is able to engage with a wide universe of institutions active in export finance, project finance and development finance. This allows us to test financing appetite early, identify realistic financing pathways and position projects before formal procurement or lender review processes are locked.
