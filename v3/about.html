Who We Are

CSM2 Finance is a financial advisory and project structuring firm focused on infrastructure, energy, industrial and government-backed development projects. We support clients where financing, technical scope, procurement strategy and implementation planning must be aligned from the beginning.

The professionals behind CSM2 Finance bring together more than 45 years of sector experience, gained through complex transactions, major infrastructure assignments and signature projects across finance, engineering, procurement and project delivery. This combined background allows CSM2 Finance to approach each mandate not only from a financing perspective, but also with a clear understanding of how projects are technically developed, procured, contracted and implemented.

Our work is shaped by senior professionals and partners with experience across international finance, EPC-F transactions, public-sector projects, engineering coordination, procurement processes and project delivery.

Our Position

Where We Stand

We do not treat financing as a separate exercise from project reality. A financing structure is only credible when the technical scope is clear, the procurement route is workable, the contract package is financeable and the implementation plan can be defended. This is where CSM2 Finance positions itself: between project owners, contractors, suppliers, lenders, ECAs, DFIs and public authorities.
