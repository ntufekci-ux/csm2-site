Financing Modalities: Integrated EPC-F Structures

We provide diverse, high-impact financing structures tailored to the unique requirements of global infrastructure and energy projects. By integrating technical maturation with strategic financial engineering, we ensure the right fit for your capital requirements and risk profile.

Sovereign Financing

Critical for delivering national-scale infrastructure in developing markets. We structure EPC-F packages that facilitate direct Ministry of Finance borrowing under sovereign guarantees. Our expertise ensures these facilities (guaranteed by top-tier ECAs) are perfectly aligned to fund strategic public assets such as gateway ports and regional road networks.

Corporate Financing

Tailored for private-sector developers and industrial giants seeking direct ECA-backed facilities. We manage complex corporate structures specifically designed to fund EPC contractors back-to-back. Our approach integrates global supply chain logistics with financial disbursement cycles, ensuring seamless payment for goods and services across the project lifecycle.

Bank Financing

Bridging capital gaps for major energy and industrial projects through creditworthy banking intermediaries. We optimize on-lending structures where commercial banks act as the borrower or guarantor of ECA-covered loans. By applying a ‘Lender’s Engineer’ lens, we ensure the project’s technical bankability meets the stringent requirements of international financial institutions.
