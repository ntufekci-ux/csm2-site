Why CSM2 Finance?

Financing does not fail only because of money. It often fails because the project is not technically mature, the procurement strategy is unclear, the contract package is not financeable, or the implementation plan is not credible.

CSM2 Finance

CSM2 Finance brings financial advisory together with engineering judgment, procurement coordination and project management experience. This allows us to identify what lenders and ECAs need to see, while also understanding what contractors, suppliers and project owners must actually deliver.
