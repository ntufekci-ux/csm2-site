Sectors Where Technical Scope and Financing Structure Must Work Together

We support capital-intensive projects in sectors where bankability depends on technical clarity, procurement discipline, reliable contracts and long-term financing structures.

Infrastructure & Transport

Roads, bridges, public facilities, transport systems and related infrastructure investments.

How we support:

project structuring, CAPEX review, procurement packaging, financing route assessment and implementation planning.

Ports & Logistics

Ports, terminals, logistics hubs, storage facilities and maritime infrastructure.

How we support:

cargo and capacity assumptions, phased development logic, EPC-F structure, lender/ECA readiness and procurement coordination.

Renewable Energy

Solar, wind, hybrid systems, storage-related infrastructure and renewable project components.

How we support:

technical and financial assumptions, EPC package review, supplier strategy, ECA eligibility, financial modelling and implementation readiness.

Industrial Facilities

Manufacturing plants, production lines, processing facilities and equipment-heavy investments.

How we support:

equipment package structuring, supplier coordination, buyer’s credit/ECA options, CAPEX validation, procurement alignment and disbursement documentation.

Water & Utilities

Water supply, wastewater, utility networks, municipal infrastructure and resilience-related investments.

How we support:

project readiness, public-sector coordination, funding route assessment, technical documentation and implementation planning.

Healthcare & Public

Hospitals, public buildings, institutional facilities and government-backed infrastructure.

How we support:

technical scope review, project packaging, procurement coordination, financing documentation and stakeholder management.

Mining & Processing

Mining-related infrastructure, processing plants, logistics and equipment packages.

How we support:

CAPEX structuring, supplier and equipment package coordination, ECA/DFI financing routes, technical risk review and implementation planning.

Development Projects

Government-backed infrastructure, public investment programmes and development-oriented projects.

How we support:

project structuring, stakeholder coordination, funding route assessment, procurement alignment, technical readiness and implementation planning.
