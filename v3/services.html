Services That Turn Projects into Financeable Transactions

Service Overview

01EPC-F & ECA/DFI Structuring

To define the most suitable financing route and transaction structure.

  • EPC-F structure development
  • ECA/DFI route assessment
  • Buyer’s credit / supplier credit / project finance options
  • Lender and ECA interface strategy
  • Transaction framework preparation
  • Financing assumptions and eligibility logic

02Financial Modelling & Bankability

To make the project understandable and assessable for lenders, ECAs and investors.

A financial model is not only an Excel file containing calculations. It is the financial translation of the project’s technical, commercial and contractual reality.

  • Financial model development/review
  • CAPEX/OPEX assumptions
  • Revenue and repayment logic
  • Sensitivity analysis
  • Financing scenarios
  • Bankability package
  • Information memorandum and lender presentation support
03Engineering & Technical Advisory

To ensure that the technical scope is mature enough to support financing discussions.

This is one of CSM2 Finance’s main differentiators. Many financial advisors can build a financing model; fewer can challenge the technical assumptions behind it.

  • Technical scope review
  • Design maturity assessment
  • CAPEX validation
  • Technical risk identification
  • Interface review between design, procurement and implementation
  • Review of technical assumptions used in financial models
  • Support to technical documentation for lenders and institutional stakeholders
04Procurement & Contract Packaging

To align procurement strategy with financing eligibility and project execution requirements.

  • Procurement strategy review
  • Supplier and contractor coordination
  • Contract packaging
  • Origin/content alignment for ECA eligibility
  • Equipment and service package structuring
  • Tender/procurement documentation support
  • Coordination between owner, EPC contractor, suppliers and financing parties
05Project Management & Implementation Readiness

To make sure the project can be implemented in line with the financing structure.

Financing parties need confidence not only in the borrower, but also in the project’s ability to be delivered.

  • Implementation strategy
  • Project execution planning
  • Schedule logic and milestone review
  • Stakeholder coordination
  • Risk register and mitigation planning
  • Reporting and project control framework
  • Coordination support during implementation preparation
06Compliance, Due Diligence & Disbursement Support

To support the administrative, documentary and integrity requirements of financed projects.

  • KYC and counterparty screening coordination
  • Integrity due diligence support
  • ECA/DFI documentation support
  • Disbursement file preparation and review
  • Compliance tracking
  • Reporting and administrative coordination
  • Coordination with specialist due diligence partners where required
