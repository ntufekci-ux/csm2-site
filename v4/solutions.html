Due diligence & business intelligence

Choose the depth the decision requires.

Five levels, from a quick counterparty screen to integrated due diligence. A risk snapshot is not a substitute for full financial, integrity, technical or legal due diligence.

1

Counterparty Risk Snapshot

A limited identity, ownership, adverse-information and business-risk screen, with dated sources and explicit gaps. Opaque ownership or material concerns are escalated.

5–7 business days, where records are available
2

Financial Due Diligence

Earnings, cash, debt, working capital and financial exposures for one entity, based on agreed periods and records.

4–6 weeks
3

KYC & Company Background

One entity, up to two jurisdictions and ten directors or owners: documented ownership, integrity and background investigation.

2–4 weeks
4

Full Integrated Due Diligence

Coordinated financial, commercial, ownership and integrity, technical, procurement, legal and tax, and ESG workstreams, with specialist sign-off and a consolidated risk and action plan.

Phased, after a scoping call
5

Enhanced Due Diligence & Business Intelligence

Targeted human-source inquiries: trade references, voluntary industry interviews, local operating context and corroboration of material business claims, assessed alongside documentary evidence.

Set after jurisdiction and source access are assessed

How we report

Each report separates established facts, source accounts and analyst judgment, and states confidence levels and unresolved contradictions. Confidential sources are protected.

Who does the work

Lawful interviews and professional reference inquiries, with vetted specialists where local language, access or regulated expertise is needed. Assessments stay independent of any later financing mandate.
